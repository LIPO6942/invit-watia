# 📜 Features List — Digital Watia Invitation (invit-watia)

This document lists all features and technical capabilities integrated into the Watia electronic invitation application.

---

## 🌸 1. Guest Experience & Visual Immersion

### ✉️ Virtual Sealed Envelope
- **3D Realistic opening animation** when the guest clicks the seal.
- **Customizable wax seal** (Basmala, Couple Initials, or Wedding Rings).
- **Personalized guest address engraved on the envelope** (e.g. *السيد علي وسيدة حرمه*).

### 🌸 Visual Effects & Atmosphere
- **Animated falling rose petals & golden dust** in the background.
- **Luxury color themes** (Royal Gold, Powder Rose, Emerald Green, Day/Night Mode).
- **Automated background music** with play/pause controls (Tunisian Malouf, Wedding March).

---

## 📅 2. Event Date, Calendar & Weather

### 📅 Google Calendar Integration & Reminders
- **1-click button** to add the main event to **Google Calendar**.
- **Individual event buttons** in the program timeline (Contract signature, Dinner, Party).
- **Auto-prefilled fields**: Event Title, Date/Time, Location, and Details.
- **Built-in notification reminders**: Automatic alerts **24h before** and **1h before** the event.

### ⏰ Real-time Countdown & Heart Clock
- **Live countdown timer** (Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds).
- **Heart-shaped analog clock** with animated hands synchronized to local device time.

### ☀️ Big Day Weather Forecast
- **Smart weather card** displaying temperature, humidity, and wind forecasts for the event location on the wedding date.

---

## 📍 3. Program & GPS Navigation

### 🗓️ Event Timeline
- Chronological timeline of wedding events (Contract ceremony, Guest reception, Photo session, Dinner, Party).
- Tailored thematic icons for each event.

### 🗺️ Leaflet Interactive Map & GPS
- Modal window with **Leaflet map widget**.
- Instant 1-click navigation buttons for **Google Maps** and **Waze**.

---

## ✍️ 4. Guestbook, Voice Messages & RSVP

### 🗳️ Nominative RSVP Confirmation
- Guests confirm attendance (Alone, with Spouse, Family, or Regret).
- Live instant update to Firebase database for admin tracking.

### 🎙️ Audio Voice Messages
- Guests can **record and send a voice message** (up to 30s) directly from their phone to the bride.
- Audio player preview before sending.

### ✍️ Guestbook & Ready-made Wish Suggestions
- Submit written wishes with **1-click pre-made wish suggestions**.
- Live wishes scroller / carousel on the page.

### 📫 Bride / Groom Private Mailbox
- Dedicated secure view (`?view=bride` or `?view=groom`) for the couple to read all private wishes and listen to voice messages.

---

## 📸 5. Interactive Photo Album (VIP Pack)

### 📸 Photo Stack (Stackable Memories Album)
- Interactive swipeable stacked photo cards.
- **Guest-specific personalization**: Option to showcase specific photos tailored for individual guests.
- Auto-hides if no photos are configured for a specific guest.

---

## 🔐 6. Admin Panel (`admin.html`)

### 🛠️ Complete Wedding Management
- **Configuration Dashboard**: Couple names, parents, date/time, locations, theme, and music selector.
- **Event Manager**: Add/Edit timeline items.
- **WhatsApp Link Generator**: Automatic generation of personalized 1-click WhatsApp invitation links.
- **RSVP & Counter Tracking**: Real-time guest response tracking and view analytics.
